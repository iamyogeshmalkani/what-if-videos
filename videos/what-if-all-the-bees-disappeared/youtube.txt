TITLE
What if all the bees disappeared? 🐝

DESCRIPTION
No, we would not all die in four years. But your fruit bowl would change.

📌 Quick facts
• About 3 in 4 of the leading food crop types get some help from animal pollinators (Klein et al. 2007)
• By volume that is about 35% of food: wheat, rice and corn need wind, not bees
• The "four years" quote is not Einstein's
• Flies, butterflies, birds and bats would still help, but food would be less varied and likely pricier
Figures are approximate; sources are in FACTS.md in the source zip.
