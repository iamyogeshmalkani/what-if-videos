TITLE
What if it never rained again? 🌧️

DESCRIPTION
The strange part: the air would keep getting wetter.

📌 Quick facts
• Rain is how fresh water comes back to land; ponds and rivers would slowly shrink
• Even the Atacama, the driest desert, gets a little rain
• About 80% of the world's cropland is watered only by rain (FAO)
• The oceans would keep evaporating, so the air would get more and more humid (a thought experiment)
Figures are approximate; sources are in FACTS.md in the source zip.
