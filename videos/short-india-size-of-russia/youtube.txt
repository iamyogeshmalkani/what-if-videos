What if India was as big as Russia?

Russia covers about 17 million square kilometres, five times India's 3.3 million. Stretch India's 1.4 billion people across Russia's area and the crowding falls from roughly 430 people per square kilometre to about 80.

📌 Quick facts
• Russia about 17.1 million km²; India about 3.29 million km² (5.2 times)
• India's population is about 1.4 billion: about 430 people per km² now, about 80 over Russia's area
• Map is schematic
Figures are approximate and rounded.
