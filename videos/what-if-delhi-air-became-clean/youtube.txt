TITLE
What if Delhi's air became clean overnight? 🌤️

DESCRIPTION
What if Delhi's air was perfectly clean tomorrow? Here is how life in the city would change.

Every winter Delhi's air quality often goes many times above the safe limit. With clean air there would be fewer asthma cases, children playing outside, schools staying open in November, fewer smog delays, and a clear view of the sky and the Red Fort.

📌 Quick facts
• Delhi winter air often reaches the CPCB "severe" band (AQI above 400)
• The CPCB "good" band is an AQI of 0 to 50
• Comparing smog to smoking cigarettes is common but approximate
Figures are approximate and rounded.
