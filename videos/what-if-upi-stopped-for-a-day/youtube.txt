TITLE
What if UPI stopped working for one whole day? 📱

DESCRIPTION
What if UPI stopped working for one whole day? Most of us barely carry cash anymore.

UPI handles billions of payments every month, from big shops to a chai stall on the roadside. If it went down, millions of people would reach the counter and not be able to pay, ATMs would have long queues, and bills, rent and online orders would stall. For many small businesses, one day without payments is a day without income.

📌 Quick facts
• UPI handles billions of payments every month (NPCI publishes the monthly volumes; check the latest)
Figures are approximate and rounded.
