TITLE
What if the Sun disappeared?

DESCRIPTION
Imagine the Sun disappearing right now. ☀️

The crazy part? We would continue seeing sunlight for approximately 8 minutes and 20 seconds.

That's how long light takes to travel from the Sun to Earth.

And gravity has a similar delay.

Space is full of things happening that we won't know about immediately.
