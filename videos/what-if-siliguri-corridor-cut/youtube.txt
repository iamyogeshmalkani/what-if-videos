TITLE
What if the Siliguri Corridor was cut? 🗺️

DESCRIPTION
What if the thin strip of land that holds India together was cut?

The Siliguri Corridor, the "Chicken's Neck", is about 20 kilometres wide at its narrowest and links the north-east to the rest of India. Nepal, Bhutan and Bangladesh sit on its sides, and China is not far. Rail, roads, pipelines and power lines all pass through it.

📌 Quick facts
• About 20 km wide at its narrowest (sources say 17 to 22 km)
• India has been building up roads, rail links and forces around the corridor and working on other routes
• Schematic map: the neck line is stylised
Figures are approximate and rounded.
