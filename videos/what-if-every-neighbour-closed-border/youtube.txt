TITLE
What if every neighbour closed its border with India? 🚧

DESCRIPTION
What if all of India's land borders closed at once?

India has over 15,000 kilometres of land border with six neighbours: Pakistan, China, Nepal, Bhutan, Bangladesh and Myanmar. Trade by road and rail would stop, and landlocked Nepal and Bhutan would feel it just as hard. But most of India's trade by volume already goes by sea, so the real damage would be regional.

📌 Quick facts
• Land border about 15,100 km; coastline about 7,500 km
• Most of India's trade by volume goes by sea
• Schematic map: the border lines are stylised and show no claims
Figures are approximate and rounded.
