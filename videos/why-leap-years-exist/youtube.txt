TITLE
Why do leap years exist? 📅

DESCRIPTION
Why does February sometimes get an extra day? Because a year is not exactly 365 days.

📌 Quick facts
• Earth takes about 365.24 days to orbit the sun
• Julius Caesar added a leap day every four years in 45 BC
• That rule was about 11 minutes a year too long, so by the 1500s the calendar had drifted about ten days
• Pope Gregory XIII fixed it in 1582: century years are leap years only if divisible by 400 (1900 was not, 2000 was)
• Britain and India adopted the Gregorian calendar in 1752
Figures are approximate; see the sources before relying on a date.
