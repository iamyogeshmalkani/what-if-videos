TITLE
What if India never invented ZERO? 0️⃣

DESCRIPTION
What if India never gave the world zero? Zero is just a circle, but try writing 1,000 without it.

Around 1,400 years ago Indian mathematicians like Brahmagupta wrote the rules for zero as a real number. Other cultures used a gap or a dot as a placeholder, and India turned it into something you can add, subtract and calculate with. Without it we might be stuck with Roman numerals, and algebra, modern mathematics and computers all lean on zero.

📌 Quick facts
• Brahmagupta described rules for zero as a number in 628 CE
• Other cultures used a gap or a dot only as a placeholder
• XLVII × CXXIII in Roman numerals is 47 × 123 = 5,781 today
Figures are approximate and rounded.
