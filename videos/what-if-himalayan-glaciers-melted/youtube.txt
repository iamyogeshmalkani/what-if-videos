What if the Himalayan glaciers melted in 10 years? 🧊

DESCRIPTION
What if the ice of the Himalayas vanished in just 10 years?

The Himalayas hold more ice than anywhere outside the poles. The Indus, Ganga and Brahmaputra start there, and hundreds of millions of people live downstream. First the melt would flood rivers; once the ice is gone, the dry-season flow would drop. Ten years is far faster than scientists expect, but the direction is real.

📌 Quick facts
• The Hindu Kush Himalaya is the largest ice store outside the polar regions, often called the Third Pole (ICIMOD)
• The Indus depends most on glacier and snow melt; the Ganga more on monsoon rain and groundwater; the Brahmaputra on a mix
• Melting in ten years is far faster than any scientific projection. Schematic map
Figures are approximate and rounded.
