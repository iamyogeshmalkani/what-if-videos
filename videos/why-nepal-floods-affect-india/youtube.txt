TITLE
Why Do Nepal's Floods Affect India? 🌊

DESCRIPTION
Heavy rainfall in Nepal can cause rivers in India to rise thousands of kilometres downstream.

Many rivers that start in the Himalayas flow from Nepal into India, including the Kosi, Gandak and Karnali. During intense monsoon rain they can carry enormous amounts of water downstream, and if their levels rise quickly, communities in northern India can face serious flooding. Floods aren't limited by international borders: what happens upstream can directly affect people downstream.

📌 Quick facts
• The Kosi, Gandak and Karnali flow from Nepal into India
• Intense monsoon rain can send huge volumes of water downstream
• Communities in northern India can face serious flooding
• River positions on the map are approximate
Figures are approximate and rounded.
