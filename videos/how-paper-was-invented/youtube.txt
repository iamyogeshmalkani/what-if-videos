TITLE
How paper changed the world 📜

DESCRIPTION
Before paper, writing was heavy, rare and expensive. Here is how paper was invented and how it travelled.

📌 Quick facts
• Cai Lun, a Chinese court official, is traditionally credited with presenting improved paper to the emperor around 105 AD, made from bark, hemp, rags and old fishing nets
• Tradition says papermaking reached Samarkand after the Battle of Talas in 751, and Baghdad soon had paper mills
• Paper mills opened in Spain by the 12th century, then Italy and the rest of Europe
• Cheap paper made cheap books possible
Figures are approximate; see the sources before relying on a date.
