TITLE
What if cricket never came to India? 🏏

DESCRIPTION
What if cricket never came to India? Would the IPL even exist?

Cricket arrived with the British and is now by far the biggest sport in the country. Without it another sport, maybe hockey, football or kabaddi, might have taken its place. Advertising money, sponsorships and TV deals would flow elsewhere, and millions of children would have idolised someone other than Tendulkar, Dhoni or Kohli.

📌 Quick facts
• Cricket arrived in India with the British in the 1700s
• India won Olympic hockey gold six times in a row from 1928 to 1956, and again in 1964 and 1980
Figures are approximate and rounded.
