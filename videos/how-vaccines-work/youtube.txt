TITLE
How vaccines beat smallpox 💉

DESCRIPTION
One disease killed an estimated 300 million people in the 20th century. This is how Edward Jenner started the fight back.

📌 Quick facts
• In May 1796 Edward Jenner used cowpox on eight-year-old James Phipps; the boy later did not fall ill when exposed to smallpox
• The word vaccine comes from the Latin word for cow, vacca
• A vaccine shows the immune system a harmless sign of the disease so the body remembers it
• In 1980 the World Health Organization declared smallpox eradicated, the first human disease wiped out
Figures are approximate; see the sources before relying on a date.
