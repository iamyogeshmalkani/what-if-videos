DESCRIPTION
What if India and China stopped trading with each other?

India and China trade about 118 billion dollars a year, and India buys far more than it sells: a gap of roughly 85 billion dollars. Phone parts, solar panels, machines, chemicals and a large share of medicine ingredients come from China. A sudden stop would hit factories first, then prices. China would lose a market too, though India is a small share of its exports.

📌 Quick facts
• 2023-24: India imported about 101 billion dollars from China and exported about 17 billion (Indian commerce ministry)
• Pharmaceutical raw ingredients: often cited at around 70% from China
• India is about 3% of China's exports. A what-if, not a prediction
Figures are approximate and rounded.
