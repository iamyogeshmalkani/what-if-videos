TITLE
What if every vehicle in India went electric tomorrow? ⚡

DESCRIPTION
What if every vehicle in India turned electric overnight? It sounds perfect, but there is a big problem.

City air would get cleaner and India would use far less imported oil. But charging every vehicle would need huge amounts of electricity, much of it still from coal. There would not be enough chargers, petrol pumps would close, and the grid, the batteries and the jobs would all have to change at once.

📌 Quick facts
• Coal supplies a little over 70 percent of India's electricity (Ministry of Power; check the latest year)
• India imports about 85 percent of its crude oil
Figures are approximate and rounded.
