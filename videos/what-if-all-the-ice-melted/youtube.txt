TITLE
What if all the ice on Earth melted? 🧊

DESCRIPTION
Floating ice barely matters. The ice on land is a different story.

📌 Quick facts
• Sea ice is already floating, so melting it barely changes sea level
• Greenland's ice would raise the seas about 7 m
• Antarctica holds about 58 m; with mountain glaciers, roughly 70 m in all (USGS)
• It would likely take thousands of years, not overnight
Figures are approximate; sources are in FACTS.md in the source zip.
