TITLE
What if India Became 5°C Colder Overnight? ❄️

DESCRIPTION
India got 5 degrees hotter. But imagine if the entire country became 5 degrees colder!

Cities like Delhi could face much harsher winters, and places that already see snow could get even more extreme cold. Agriculture could change dramatically: some crops might benefit from cooler conditions while others face frost damage. Heating demand would rise, transport could be disrupted in colder regions, and ecosystems would have to adapt. Five degrees sounds small, but across a whole country it's enormous.

📌 Quick facts
• A thought experiment: every part of India 5°C colder
• Harsher winters in the north, more extreme cold where it already snows
• Some crops could benefit, others could face frost damage
• More energy for heating, possible transport disruption
Figures are approximate and rounded.
