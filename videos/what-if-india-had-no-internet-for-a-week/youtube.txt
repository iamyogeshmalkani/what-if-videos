TITLE
What if India had no internet for 7 days? 📵

DESCRIPTION
What if India had no internet for seven days? No WhatsApp, no UPI, no maps.

You could not book a cab or order food. Offices that depend on email and video calls would stop, schools with online classes would close, and small businesses that sell online would lose a full week of sales. Hospitals, banks and airlines also depend on networks, so a lot would slow down.

📌 Quick facts
• Everyday services like payments, maps, messaging and cloud tools all depend on the internet
A thought experiment, not a forecast.
