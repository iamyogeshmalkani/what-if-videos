TITLE
What if ALL of India lived in Mumbai? 🤯

DESCRIPTION
What if all 1.4 billion Indians lived in Mumbai? It would be over 100 times more crowded than it is today.

Mumbai city covers only about 600 square kilometres and is already one of the most crowded cities on Earth. In this short we look at what would happen to homes, local trains, water supply and the city limits if the whole country moved in.

📌 Quick facts
• Mumbai city: about 603 square kilometres (BMC)
• India: about 1.4 billion people
• That works out to roughly 2.3 million people per square kilometre, against about 20,000 in Mumbai today
Figures are approximate and rounded.
