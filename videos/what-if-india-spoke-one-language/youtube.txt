TITLE
What if ALL of India spoke ONE language? 🗣️

DESCRIPTION
What if all of India spoke just one language? India has 22 official languages, and hundreds more spoken in homes, markets and villages.

One language would make exams, courts and apps work the same everywhere, and a farmer in Assam could talk to a doctor in Kerala. But a language also carries songs, jokes, proverbs and stories, many states feel strongly about whose language it would be, and millions of people would have to relearn how to read, write and do business almost overnight.

📌 Quick facts
• India has 22 officially recognised languages
• A rupee note prints its value in 15 languages
• Hundreds more languages are spoken in homes, markets and villages
Figures are approximate and rounded.
