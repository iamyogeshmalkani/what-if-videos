TITLE
What if Taiwan's chips were cut off? 💻

DESCRIPTION
What if the island that makes most of the world's most advanced chips was cut off?

Taiwan makes roughly 90 percent of the world's most advanced chips, most of them at TSMC, and phones, cars, servers and AI all depend on them. The Taiwan Strait is about 130 kilometres wide at its narrowest. A blockade or a conflict could stop shipments; analysts put the cost of a Taiwan crisis in the trillions of dollars. India's first chip plants start with older chips and assembly.

📌 Quick facts
• About 90% of the most advanced chips; TSMC is well over half of global foundry revenue
• India's first plants: Tata-PSMC at Dholera and Micron's assembly plant at Sanand
• Not a claim about any country's intentions. A what-if, not a prediction
Figures are approximate and rounded.
