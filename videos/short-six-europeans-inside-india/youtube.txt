DESCRIPTION
What if six European giants fit inside India, with room left over?

France, Germany, Spain, Italy, the UK and Poland together cover roughly 2.3 million square kilometres. India covers about 3.3 million, which leaves around a million free, about the area of Egypt. And India has more people than all of Europe.

📌 Quick facts
• Areas (km²): France mainland about 551,000, Germany 357,000, Spain 506,000, Italy 301,000, UK 244,000, Poland 313,000
• India about 3.29 million km²; Egypt about 1.0 million km²
• Map is schematic
Figures are approximate and rounded.
