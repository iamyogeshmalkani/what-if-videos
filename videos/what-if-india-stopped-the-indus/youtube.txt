TITLE
What if India stopped the Indus? 💧

DESCRIPTION
What if India closed the tap on the rivers Pakistan's farms depend on?

The 1960 Indus Waters Treaty gave India three eastern rivers and Pakistan three western ones. In 2025, India placed the treaty in abeyance after the Pahalgam attack. Pakistan relies on the Indus system for most of its irrigated farmland, but India has limited storage on the western rivers: it can delay or regulate flows, not stop them quickly.

📌 Quick facts
• Eastern rivers (India): Ravi, Beas, Sutlej. Western rivers (Pakistan): Indus, Jhelum, Chenab
• India placed the treaty in abeyance in April 2025; the dispute is now in international law (Court of Arbitration)
• Building large storage takes years
• Schematic map: no borders are drawn and the river lines are stylised
