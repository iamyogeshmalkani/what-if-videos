TITLE
India Had Villages Completely Surrounded by Bangladesh! 🗺️

DESCRIPTION
Imagine living in an Indian village… but to reach the rest of India, you had to cross Bangladesh!

This actually happened along the India-Bangladesh border. Unusual territories called enclaves meant some Indian villages were completely surrounded by Bangladeshi territory, and some Bangladeshi villages by India. For generations these communities faced problems with access, administration and basic services. In 2015 India and Bangladesh exchanged enclaves under a historic land boundary agreement.

📌 Quick facts
• Enclaves are territories completely surrounded by another country
• In 2015 India and Bangladesh exchanged enclaves under a land boundary agreement
• 111 Indian enclaves went to Bangladesh
• 51 Bangladeshi enclaves became part of India; patch positions on the map are illustrative
Figures are approximate and rounded.
