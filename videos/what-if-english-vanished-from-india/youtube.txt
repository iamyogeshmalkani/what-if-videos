TITLE
What if English Disappeared From India Tomorrow? 🗣️

DESCRIPTION
Imagine waking up tomorrow and nobody in India could speak English.

English is deeply embedded in courts, universities, international business and millions of workplaces. Government communication, legal documents and technical education would need major adjustments. But India wouldn't become silent: we have hundreds of languages, each with its own history and identity. The real challenge would be replacing one common communication bridge across so many communities.

📌 Quick facts
• English is used in courts, universities, businesses and workplaces
• India has hundreds of languages
• Languages shown on the map (Hindi, Bengali, Tamil and others) are examples
• A thought experiment, not a prediction
Figures are approximate and rounded.
