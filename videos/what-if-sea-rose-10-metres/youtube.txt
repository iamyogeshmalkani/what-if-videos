TITLE
What if the sea rose 10 metres? Mumbai or Kolkata? 🌊

DESCRIPTION
What if the sea rose 10 metres and India's coastline was redrawn?

The Sundarbans, shared with Bangladesh, would go first because they are barely above sea level. Kolkata sits only a few metres above the sea, and Mumbai's southern end is built largely on reclaimed land. Ten metres is an extreme case, but even small rises make cyclone floods worse today.

📌 Quick facts
• Sundarbans delta: barely above sea level
• Kolkata: only a few metres above sea level; south Mumbai: largely reclaimed land
• Ten metres is far beyond current projections for this century. The blue areas are schematic, not a flood model
Figures are approximate and rounded.
