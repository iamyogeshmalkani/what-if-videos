TITLE
What if CHAI disappeared from India? ☕

DESCRIPTION
What if chai vanished from India overnight? India drinks most of the tea it grows, and every morning hundreds of millions of cups are poured.

Roadside chai sellers outside offices, bus stands and railway stations would lose their only income. Tea estates in Assam, Darjeeling and the Nilgiris, and the more than a million workers who depend on them, would be hit next. And the tapri conversation, the office break and the long train wait would go quiet.

📌 Quick facts
• India drinks most of the tea it grows
• More than a million workers depend on tea estates in Assam, Darjeeling and the Nilgiris
• A century ago most Indians did not drink tea every day; it became the national drink in a few generations
Figures are approximate and rounded.
