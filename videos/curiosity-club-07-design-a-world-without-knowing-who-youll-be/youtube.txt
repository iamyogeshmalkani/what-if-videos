TITLE
Design a world without knowing who you'll be

DESCRIPTION
Imagine creating the rules of the world without knowing where you'll be born.

You could be rich or poor. Powerful or powerless. Healthy or disabled.

Would you still design the same society?

This thought experiment, inspired by philosopher John Rawls, challenges how we think about fairness, equality, and justice.

If you could redesign the world, what is the first rule you'd change?
