TITLE
Which Indian state is as big as a country? 🏞️

DESCRIPTION
What if an Indian state was a country? Rajasthan is bigger than the entire United Kingdom.

Rajasthan covers about 342,000 square kilometres; the UK about 244,000. Maharashtra is slightly larger than Italy, with more than twice the people. Uttar Pradesh has more people than Brazil.

📌 Quick facts
• Rajasthan about 342,000 km²; UK about 244,000 km²
• Maharashtra about 308,000 km² and about 126 million people; Italy about 301,000 km² and about 59 million people
• Uttar Pradesh about 240 million people; Brazil about 213 million
Figures are approximate and rounded.
