TITLE
What if the Sundarbans Vanished Tomorrow? 🌿

DESCRIPTION
What if India's natural shield against coastal storms suddenly disappeared?

This is the Sundarbans, the world's largest mangrove forest, shared by India and Bangladesh and home to the famous Royal Bengal Tiger. Its roots help stabilise shorelines, trap sediments and reduce the impact of waves and storm surges. Without the mangroves, coastal communities could become more vulnerable to erosion, flooding and storms.

📌 Quick facts
