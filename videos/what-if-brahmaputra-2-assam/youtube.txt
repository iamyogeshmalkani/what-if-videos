TITLE
What if China blocked the Brahmaputra? Part 2: Assam 🌧️

DESCRIPTION
What if a big dam upstream hit Assam? Assam already floods every single year.

A big dam stores water and releases it later. In the dry season less may arrive, and farms, fisheries and river ports feel it first. In the monsoon, a sudden release could add a flood surge on top of normal rain. The risk is less about cutting off and more about timing.

📌 Quick facts
• Assam floods in most monsoons (the Assam State Disaster Management Authority publishes yearly figures)
• India has asked China for early warning and river data; check the latest status of any data-sharing arrangement
• The map is schematic
Figures are approximate and rounded.
