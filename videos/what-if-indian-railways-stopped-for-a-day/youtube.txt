TITLE
What if every train in India stopped for one day? 🚆

DESCRIPTION
What if every train in India stopped for 24 hours? Over 20 million passengers would be stranded.

That is nearly the population of Australia, every single day. Workers could not reach offices, stations would fill with stranded travellers, and students would miss exams. Then the freight: trains carry coal, grain, fertiliser and fuel, and power plants depend on coal wagons arriving on time, so supply chains would begin to stall within hours.

📌 Quick facts
• Indian Railways carries about 24 million passengers a day (Ministry of Railways; depends on the year and on whether suburban trips are counted)
• Australia has about 27 million people (ABS)
Figures are approximate and rounded.
