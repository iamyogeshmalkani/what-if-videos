TITLE
Why does the Moon change shape? 🌙

DESCRIPTION
The Moon never changes shape. What changes is how much of its lit half we can see.

📌 Quick facts
• Half of the Moon is always lit by the sun
• The phases cycle takes about 29.5 days
• Earth's shadow causes lunar eclipses, not the phases
• The Moon spins once per orbit, so we always see the same face; the far side is not permanently dark
Figures are approximate; see the sources before relying on a date.
