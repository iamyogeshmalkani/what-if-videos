TITLE
What if the Strait of Hormuz closed? ⛽

DESCRIPTION
What if the narrow strait that carries a fifth of the world's oil closed?

The Strait of Hormuz is about 33 kilometres wide at its narrowest, between Iran and Oman. India imports most of its crude oil and a big part comes from the Gulf. Close the strait and prices would jump within hours; India's emergency reserve covers only about ten days of demand. Pipelines bypass the strait, but not enough to replace it.

📌 Quick facts
• About a fifth of world oil consumption and a large share of Qatar's LNG pass through Hormuz (US EIA)
• India imports about 85% of its crude; its strategic reserve holds roughly 9 to 10 days of demand
• Saudi Arabia's East-West pipeline and the UAE's Habshan-Fujairah pipeline bypass the strait. A what-if, not a prediction; routes are stylised
Figures are approximate and rounded.
