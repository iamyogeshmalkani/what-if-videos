TITLE
This Ancient Indian City Had Something Modern Cities Still Struggle With 🏺

DESCRIPTION
4,500 years ago, this city had a drainage system that would surprise you even today.

This is Mohenjo-daro, one of the greatest cities of the ancient Indus Valley Civilisation. It had carefully planned streets, houses connected to drainage systems, covered drains running alongside roads, and many houses even had bathing areas. It was an organised urban civilisation, and much of its engineering is still being studied today.

📌 Quick facts
• Mohenjo-daro is an Indus Valley Civilisation city, about 4,500 years old
• Planned streets, house drains and covered drains along roads are well documented
• Many houses had bathing areas
• The plan shown on screen is a simplified drawing, not a survey
Figures are approximate and rounded.
