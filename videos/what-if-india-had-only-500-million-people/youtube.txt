TITLE
What if India's Population Was Only 500 Million? 👥

DESCRIPTION
India has over 1.4 billion people. Now imagine removing almost two-thirds of them!

Instead of one of the world's most populated countries, India would have just 500 million people. There could be less pressure on housing, roads, public transport and some natural resources, and cities might have more space. But India would also have a smaller workforce, fewer consumers and a very different economy. A smaller population doesn't automatically mean a richer country.

📌 Quick facts
• India has over 1.4 billion people
• About 500 million is a round figure for removing roughly two-thirds
• Less pressure on housing, roads, transport and resources
• A smaller workforce and fewer consumers would change the economy
Figures are approximate and rounded.
