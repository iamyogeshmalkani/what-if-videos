TITLE
What if China blocked the Brahmaputra? Part 1: The river 🏞️

DESCRIPTION
What if China blocked the Brahmaputra? Part 1 follows the river from Tibet to the Bay of Bengal.

China's planned hydropower project near the Great Bend is reported to be far bigger than the Three Gorges Dam. But most of the river's monsoon water is added inside India, by rain and tributaries, so a dam cannot simply switch it off. It could change when and how much water arrives.

📌 Quick facts
• One river, four names: Yarlung Tsangpo (Tibet), Siang (Arunachal Pradesh), Brahmaputra (Assam), Jamuna and Meghna (Bangladesh)
• The lower Yarlung Tsangpo (Great Bend, Medog) project was approved in December 2024 and is widely reported at about 60,000 MW, against 22,500 MW for Three Gorges
• The river line on the map is stylised, not surveyed
Figures are approximate and rounded.
