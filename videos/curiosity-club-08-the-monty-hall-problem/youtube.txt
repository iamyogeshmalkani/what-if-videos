TITLE
The Monty Hall problem

DESCRIPTION
3 doors. 1 car. 2 goats. 🚪

You make your choice. The host opens another door and reveals a goat.

Now you can switch or stay.

Here's the surprising part: switching gives you a 2/3 chance of winning, while staying gives you only 1/3.

Your intuition might disagree, but the mathematics doesn't.

Be honest: Would you switch?
