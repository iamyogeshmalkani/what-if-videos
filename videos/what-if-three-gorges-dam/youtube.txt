TITLE
What if the Three Gorges Dam collapsed? 🌊

DESCRIPTION
What if the biggest power station on Earth failed? A wall of water would race down the Yangtze towards cities where hundreds of millions of people live.

The Three Gorges Dam holds back a reservoir about 600 kilometres long. This is a what-if, not a prediction: engineers built it with flood control in mind and experts call a collapse extremely unlikely.

📌 Quick facts
• Installed capacity: 22,500 megawatts, the largest power station in the world (China Three Gorges Corporation)
• The wall is about 2,335 metres long
• Roughly 400 million people live in the Yangtze basin, about a third of China
