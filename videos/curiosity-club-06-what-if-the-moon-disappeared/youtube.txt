TITLE
What if the Moon disappeared?

DESCRIPTION
The Moon is doing much more than making our nights beautiful. 🌕

It influences ocean tides and helps stabilize Earth's axial tilt over long periods.

If it suddenly disappeared, Earth wouldn't end overnight. But our planet would slowly become a very different place.

Sometimes, the things we take for granted are more important than we realise.

Could you imagine a night sky without the Moon?
