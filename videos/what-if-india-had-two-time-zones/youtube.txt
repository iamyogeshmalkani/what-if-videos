TITLE
What if India had TWO time zones? ⏰

DESCRIPTION
What if India had two time zones? Right now the whole country runs on one clock, but the sun rises almost two hours earlier in the northeast than in Gujarat.

In Assam, summer mornings are bright before 5 and tea gardens already run their own "garden time", an hour ahead. Two zones could mean more daylight at work and, some studies say, lower electricity use. But train timetables, flights, banks and exams would need new rules, and a confused clock on a busy single-line track can be dangerous.

📌 Quick facts
• India is nearly 3,000 kilometres wide from east to west
• The sun rises almost two hours earlier in the northeast than in Gujarat
• Russia uses 11 time zones; China, about as wide as India, uses one
Figures are approximate and rounded.
