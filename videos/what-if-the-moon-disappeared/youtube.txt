TITLE
What if the Moon disappeared? 🌙

DESCRIPTION
Nothing would explode, but the ocean would notice first.

📌 Quick facts
• The Moon drives most of our tides; without it, tides would shrink to less than half (the Sun alone)
• Every night would be as dark as a new moon
• Over millions of years, Earth's tilt could wobble much more, so seasons could swing to extremes (models differ on how much)
Figures are approximate; sources are in FACTS.md in the source zip.
