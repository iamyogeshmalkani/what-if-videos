TITLE
What if Earth stopped spinning? 🌍

DESCRIPTION
The ground under the equator is moving faster than a jet.

📌 Quick facts
• The equator moves east at about 1,670 km/h (about 1,000 mph)
• A sudden stop would send everything not tied down flying east
• A gentle stop: one day would last a year, six months of sun, six months of night
• Models suggest the oceans would drain toward the poles and a giant continent would rise at the equator
• Without spin, hurricanes could not swirl, and the magnetic field might weaken
Figures are approximate; sources are in FACTS.md in the source zip.
