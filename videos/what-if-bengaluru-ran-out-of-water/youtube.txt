TITLE
What if Bengaluru Ran Out of Water Tomorrow? 💧

DESCRIPTION
Imagine Bengaluru waking up tomorrow with absolutely no water coming from its taps.

Bengaluru depends on several water sources, including the Cauvery River and groundwater, and its growing population puts enormous pressure on supply. Now imagine a long drought combined with falling groundwater levels: apartments, offices, restaurants and industries would all compete for limited water, tankers would get costlier and some businesses might struggle. A city can grow with more buildings and roads, but without water none of it works.

📌 Quick facts
• Bengaluru draws on the Cauvery River and groundwater
• A growing population puts pressure on water supply
• A thought experiment about a prolonged drought
• Water tankers would become more expensive
Figures are approximate and rounded.
