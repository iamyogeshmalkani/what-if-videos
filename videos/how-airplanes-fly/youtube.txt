TITLE
How do planes stay up? ✈️

DESCRIPTION
A jumbo jet weighs hundreds of tons, so how does it stay in the air? The secret is the wing.

📌 Quick facts
• A tilted, curved wing pushes air downward, and the air pushes the wing up
• Faster air above the wing means lower pressure there, which also helps lift
• Lift only works with air moving past the wing, so planes need engines and a runway
• On 17 December 1903 the Wright brothers' first flight at Kitty Hawk lasted 12 seconds and covered about 120 feet
Figures are approximate; see the sources before relying on a date.
