TITLE
Why do we say tea or chai? 🍵

DESCRIPTION
Almost every language says tea in one of two ways. The reason is how tea travelled.

📌 Quick facts
• Overland across the Silk Road, the Chinese word cha spread: Persian chay, Turkish çay, Russian chay, Hindi chai
• By sea from Fujian, the word te was carried to Europe by Dutch traders in the early 1600s: English tea, German Tee, French thé
• Portugal is the exception: its traders bought from Macau, where the word was cha, so they say chá
• Rule of thumb: ch means it travelled by land, t means by sea
Figures are approximate; see the sources before relying on a date.
