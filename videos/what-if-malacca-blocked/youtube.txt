TITLE
What if the Strait of Malacca was blocked? 🚢

DESCRIPTION
What if the strait that links the Indian Ocean to the Pacific was blocked?

The Strait of Malacca is only about 2.7 kilometres wide at its narrowest, near Singapore. Roughly a quarter of the world's traded goods and a large share of China's oil imports sail through it, and India's Andaman and Nicobar Islands sit at its western entrance. Ships could reroute through the longer Sunda and Lombok straits, and China is building pipelines in Myanmar and Pakistan to cut the risk.

📌 Quick facts
• About 800 km long and about 2.7 km wide at the Phillip Channel
• Indira Point (Great Nicobar) is about 150 km from Sumatra
• Blocking an international strait would be an act of war. A what-if, not a prediction; routes are stylised
Figures are approximate and rounded.
