TITLE
Why is the sky blue? The real reason 🌤️

DESCRIPTION
Sunlight looks white, so why is the sky blue? The answer is in how air scatters light.

📌 Quick facts
• Sunlight is a mix of all colours
• Short waves (blue) scatter much more than long waves (red) off air molecules — called Rayleigh scattering, worked out by Lord Rayleigh in 1871
• Violet scatters even more, but the sun gives off less of it, the air absorbs some and our eyes see it poorly
• At sunset the light crosses more air, so the blue is scattered away and orange and red are left
Figures are approximate; see the sources before relying on a date.
