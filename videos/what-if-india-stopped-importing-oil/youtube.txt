TITLE
What if India stopped importing oil? ⛽

DESCRIPTION
What if India stopped buying oil from other countries? Petrol pumps would run dry within weeks.

India imports roughly 85 percent of the crude oil it uses, and almost every bus, truck, bike and car runs on it. Without imports, trucks carrying vegetables, milk and grain would slow down, factories would cut production, flights would be cancelled, and prices of everything would climb.

📌 Quick facts
• India imports roughly 85% of its crude oil (Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas; recent years have been 85 to 88%)
• Buses, trucks, bikes and cars run mostly on petrol and diesel made from crude
Figures are approximate and rounded.
