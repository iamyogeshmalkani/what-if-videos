TITLE
Do people on higher floors age faster?

DESCRIPTION
Your neighbour on the top floor is technically ageing faster than you. ⏳

According to Einstein's theory of general relativity, time passes slightly faster where gravity is weaker.

The higher you go, the farther you are from Earth's centre, and the weaker gravity becomes.

The difference is incredibly tiny, but scientists can actually measure it using atomic clocks.

Time isn't the same everywhere. Even on Earth.
