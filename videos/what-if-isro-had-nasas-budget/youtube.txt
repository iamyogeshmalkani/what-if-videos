TITLE
What if ISRO had NASA's budget? 🚀

DESCRIPTION
What if ISRO had as much money as NASA? India's Mars mission cost less than some Hollywood movies.

ISRO already does so much with so little. With NASA-level funding it could build bigger rockets, send more astronauts deeper into space, and launch many more satellites for farming, weather and communication, becoming a top space power much faster.

📌 Quick facts
• Mangalyaan (Mars Orbiter Mission) cost about 450 crore rupees, around 74 million US dollars
• NASA's yearly budget is about 25 billion dollars (FY2024)
• ISRO's yearly budget is about 1.6 billion dollars (about 13,000 crore rupees)
Figures are approximate and rounded.
