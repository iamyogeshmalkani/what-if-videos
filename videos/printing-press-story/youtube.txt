TITLE
The machine that spread ideas: the printing press 📖

DESCRIPTION
Five hundred years ago a single book could cost more than a house. Then Gutenberg's press changed everything.

📌 Quick facts
• Before about 1450, European books were copied by hand
• Johannes Gutenberg developed movable metal type, oil-based ink and a press adapted from wine presses; his Bible was printed around 1455
• Movable type had existed earlier in China and Korea
• By 1500 Europe had printed millions of books
• Martin Luther's arguments spread widely in print from 1517
Figures are approximate; see the sources before relying on a date.
