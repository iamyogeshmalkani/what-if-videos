TITLE
What if every Indian state was its own country? 🇮🇳

DESCRIPTION
What if every Indian state was its own country? India would split into 28 nations, and one state would suddenly be bigger than Brazil.

In 1947 over 500 princely states could have said no to India, and Travancore (today's Kerala) actually declared itself independent for about six weeks. So we flip it: what if every state said no? Uttar Pradesh would outgrow Brazil, Maharashtra would keep Mumbai, your office in Gurugram would be in a different country, and the IPL would become a World Cup.

📌 Quick facts
• India has 28 states and 8 union territories
• At independence in 1947 there were over 500 princely states (565 is the usual count)
• Travancore (mostly today's Kerala) announced it would stay independent and joined India about six weeks later
• Uttar Pradesh: about 241 million people (2024 estimate); Brazil: about 212 million (UN World Population Prospects 2024)
• Maharashtra holds Mumbai, India's financial capital; Gurugram is in Haryana, next to Delhi
• The map uses the Survey of India outline
Figures are approximate and rounded.
