TITLE
The forgotten city of the Indus Valley 🏺

DESCRIPTION
Four and a half thousand years ago a city had drains in every street. Meet the Indus Valley Civilization.

📌 Quick facts
• From about 2600 BC it spread across present-day Pakistan and north-west India
• Cities such as Harappa and Mohenjo-daro had grid streets, baked-brick houses, drains and standard weights
• Lothal in Gujarat has a dockyard-like structure
• Its script has still not been deciphered
• The cities declined from about 1900 BC; the causes are debated, and they were rediscovered in the 1920s
Figures are approximate; see the sources before relying on a date.
