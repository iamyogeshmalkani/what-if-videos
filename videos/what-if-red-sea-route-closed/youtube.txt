What if the Red Sea shipping route closed for good?

The Suez Canal and Red Sea carry about 12 percent of world trade. Since late 2023, attacks on ships pushed many carriers round the Cape of Good Hope, which adds roughly ten days and thousands of kilometres. For India that means higher freight, delays and insurance costs, and the Indian Navy sent warships to guard merchant ships in the Arabian Sea.

📌 Quick facts
• Suez and the Red Sea: about 12% of world trade and roughly 30% of container traffic
• The Cape detour adds about 10 days on Asia-Europe voyages
• A what-if, not a prediction; routes are stylised
Figures are approximate and rounded.
