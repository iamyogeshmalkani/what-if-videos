TITLE
Would you sacrifice one to save five?

DESCRIPTION
A runaway train is heading towards five people.

You can pull a lever and save them.

But doing so would kill someone on the other track.

And this time, that person is your sibling.

Would you pull the lever?

The Trolley Problem challenges our ideas about morality, responsibility, and the value of human life.

There is no easy answer.

Tell me what you would actually do. Not what sounds morally correct.
