TITLE
What if the Sun disappeared right now? ☀️

DESCRIPTION
For about eight minutes, you would not even notice.

📌 Quick facts
• Sunlight and gravity both take about 8 minutes 20 seconds to reach Earth
• The Moon and planets would go dark too: they only reflect sunlight
• Within about a week the average temperature could fall below −18 °C (estimate)
• The seas would freeze on top, but deep oceans could stay liquid for a very long time
• With nothing to orbit, Earth would drift off in a straight line
Figures are approximate; sources are in FACTS.md in the source zip.
