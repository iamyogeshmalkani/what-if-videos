TITLE
What if every FARMER in India stopped for a WEEK? 🌾

DESCRIPTION
What if every farmer in India stopped working for a week? Nearly half of India’s workforce is in farming, and they grow the food for about 1.4 billion people.

Grain stocks in government godowns would last a while. The fresh things would go first: milk, vegetables and fruit do not wait. By day two the mandis would be empty and city prices would jump, and crops left unwatered or unharvested would start to spoil.

📌 Quick facts
• Nearly half of India’s workforce works in farming
• In June 2018 farmers in several states held a ten-day protest and some cities felt shortages of milk and vegetables
• A nationwide stoppage would be far bigger
Figures are approximate and rounded.
